# Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

**1.1** This policy explains how employees may use Northwind Air computers, networks and accounts.

**1.2** It applies to everyone who is given access to company technology.

## 2. Passwords and accounts

**2.1** Passwords must be at least 10 characters long.

**2.2** Users must sign out of shared computers when they leave them.

**2.3** Accounts are locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts.

**2.4** Locked accounts are unlocked by the IT service desk after the user's identity is checked.

## 3. Software and equipment

**3.1** Company equipment is for business use. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect work or security.

**3.2** Employees may not install unapproved software on company devices.

**3.3** Equipment must be returned to IT when an employee leaves the company.

## 4. Internet and email

**4.1** Employees must not use company systems to view, store or send illegal or offensive material.

**4.2** Company email must not be forwarded automatically to personal email accounts.

**4.3** Attachments and links from unknown senders should be reported to the IT service desk before they are opened.

## 5. Reporting problems

**5.1** Employees must report a lost or stolen device to the IT service desk immediately.

**5.2** Faults in company equipment are reported through the service desk portal.

## 6. Related policies

**6.1** For personal device rules, see the Bring Your Own Device Policy IT-007.

**6.2** For the rules on protecting company information, see the Information Security Policy.
