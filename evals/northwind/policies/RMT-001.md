# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

**1.1** This policy explains when and how Northwind Air employees may work away from company premises.

**1.2** It applies to office-based roles. Roles that must be performed at an airport, hangar or aircraft are not covered.

## 2. Eligibility and frequency

**2.1** Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week without approval.

**2.2** Teams may set days when everyone is expected in the office for meetings and workshops.

**2.3** Remote work arrangements can be changed by the company when business needs change.

## 3. Work location

**3.1** Remote work should be done from the employee's home address on record.

**3.2** Remote work from outside Canada requires approval from both Human Resources and Legal.

**3.3** Employees must tell Human Resources if they move to a different province.

## 4. Security while working remotely

**4.1** Multi-factor authentication is required for remote access to company systems.

**4.2** Employees may access company email from a personal phone, provided the company device management app is installed.

**4.3** Company files must be stored in approved cloud storage, not on personal devices.

**4.4** Employees must not work on confidential documents in public places where the screen can be seen.

## 5. Equipment and costs

**5.1** Employees receive a home office equipment allowance of CAD 500 once every 3 years.

**5.2** The company provides a laptop and a headset to every employee approved for remote work.

**5.3** Home internet and utility costs are not reimbursed.
