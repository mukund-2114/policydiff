# Information Security Policy

## 1. Purpose, scope and definitions

**1.1** This policy protects the information and systems that Northwind Air relies on.

**1.2** It applies to all employees, contractors and third parties who use company systems.

**1.3** Confidential data means customer personal data and financial records.

## 2. Accounts and passwords

**2.1** Every user must have a unique account. Shared accounts are not allowed.

**2.2** Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.

**2.3** Passwords must not be shared or written down where others can see them.

**2.4** Accounts are locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts.

## 3. Devices

**3.1** Employees must not use personal devices to access company email.

**3.2** Company laptops and phones must use full disk encryption.

**3.3** Devices must install security updates when they are released by IT.

## 4. Access control

**4.1** Multi-factor authentication is required for remote access to company systems.

**4.2** Access rights are reviewed every quarter by the system owner.

**4.3** Software may only be installed after approval from IT.

## 5. Security incidents

**5.1** Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk immediately.

**5.2** Employees must not try to investigate a suspected security incident on their own.

**5.3** Security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Centre within 1 hour of discovery.

## 6. Travel and remote locations

**6.1** Laptops taken on trips must be encrypted and switched off when passing through border controls.

**6.2** Working from outside Canada requires approval from HR and Legal.

## 7. Data deletion

**7.1** Data is deleted according to the retention schedule published by Compliance.

**7.2** IT Security approves all deletion of customer data before it is carried out.

## 8. Retired policies

**8.1** Bring Your Own Device Policy IT-007 was retired on 1 February 2025. Rules for personal devices are now in section 3 of this policy.
