# Employee Handbook Summary

## 1. Purpose

**1.1** This summary gives new and existing employees a short guide to benefits, working arrangements and conduct at Northwind Air.

**1.2** Where a full policy exists, the full policy applies and this summary points to it.

## 2. Time off

**2.1** Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per year.

**2.2** Public holidays follow the law of the province where the employee works.

**2.3** Vacation requests should be submitted to the line manager well before the planned dates.

**2.4** Employees may carry over up to 5 unused vacation days to the following year.

## 3. Sick leave

**3.1** Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days each year.

**3.2** Employees should tell their line manager as early as possible when they are unwell.

## 4. Family leave

**4.1** Family leave includes bereavement, parental and caregiver leave.

**4.2** Employees may take up to 3 paid days of bereavement leave for an immediate family member.

**4.3** Caregiver leave is unpaid and must be agreed with the line manager.

**4.4** Parental leave is topped up by the company for 16 weeks.

## 5. Working arrangements

**5.1** All remote work requires prior written approval from the employee's department head.

**5.2** Employees working on site must follow the building access rules.

**5.3** Core hours are agreed by each team and recorded by the line manager.

## 6. Employee records

**6.1** Employees can ask Human Resources to correct their personal details at any time.

**6.2** Personnel files are retained for 7 years after employment ends.

## 7. Conduct

**7.1** Gifts to customers valued over CAD 100 must be recorded in the gift register.

**7.2** Employees must declare any conflict of interest to their line manager as soon as they become aware of it.

**7.3** Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated and can be reported to Human Resources in confidence.
