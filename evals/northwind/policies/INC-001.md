# Incident Reporting Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

**1.1** This policy explains how Northwind Air staff report incidents so that they can be investigated and prevented.

**1.2** It applies to all employees, contractors and crew.

## 2. Safety incidents

**2.1** Safety incidents must be reported within 24 hours.

**2.2** Reports are made through the incident portal. A phone report must be followed by a portal entry.

**2.3** Reporters are protected from retaliation when they report in good faith.

## 3. Other incidents

**3.1** All other incidents, including cyber incidents, must be reported within 24 hours of discovery.

**3.2** Incidents involving customer personal data must also be sent to the privacy officer.

## 4. Investigation

**4.1** Each incident is assigned an investigator who is independent of the area involved.

**4.2** The investigator records the cause, the actions taken and any follow-up work.

## 5. Related policies

**5.1** Staff in Finance and Procurement should also read the Expense Policy when an incident involves a payment.

**5.2** Safety incidents are handled under Safety Policy SAF-002.

## 6. Legal matters

**6.1** Legal counsel must be told about any incident that may lead to a claim or an investigation by a regulator.

**6.2** A legal hold overrides all retention schedules.
