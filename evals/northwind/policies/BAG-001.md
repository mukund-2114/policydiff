# Baggage Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

**1.1** This policy sets out the baggage that passengers may bring on Northwind Air flights.

**1.2** It applies to all passengers on all Northwind Air scheduled flights.

## 2. Checked baggage

**2.1** The checked baggage allowance depends on the fare class and is listed in Table 2.1.

**Table 2.1** Checked baggage allowance by fare class

| Fare class | Bags | Weight per bag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Economy | 1 | 23 kg |
| Premium Economy | 2 | 23 kg |
| Business | 2 | 32 kg |

**2.2** Each checked bag must have a name tag with the passenger's contact details.

## 3. Cabin baggage

**3.1** Each passenger may bring one cabin bag and one personal item such as a handbag or laptop bag.

**3.2** The largest cabin bag size that fits in the overhead locker is shown in Figure 3.1.

![Maximum cabin bag dimensions](figures/cabin_bag_dimensions.png)

**Figure 3.1** Maximum cabin bag dimensions

**3.3** Gate agents may ask a passenger to check a cabin bag if the overhead lockers are full.

## 4. Excess and overweight baggage

**4.1** The excess baggage fee is CAD 100 per bag above the allowance.

**4.2** Bags that weigh more than the limit for the fare class must be repacked or paid for as excess baggage.

## 5. Special items

**5.1** Sports equipment such as skis or golf clubs is accepted as checked baggage and counts towards the allowance.

**5.2** Musical instruments may be carried in the cabin if they fit under the seat or in the overhead locker.

**5.3** Dangerous goods are not permitted in checked or cabin baggage.

## 6. Damaged and delayed baggage

**6.1** Passengers must report damaged or delayed baggage to the airline before leaving the baggage hall.

**6.2** The airline will trace delayed baggage and deliver it to the passenger's address.
