# Procurement Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

**1.1** This policy sets the rules for buying goods and services for Northwind Air.

**1.2** It applies to every purchase made with company funds, whether by a department, a project team or an individual employee.

## 2. Principles

**2.1** Purchases must be competitive, documented and made in the interest of the company.

**2.2** Employees must declare any personal interest in a supplier before taking part in a purchasing decision.

## 3. Competitive quotes

**3.1** Contracts over CAD 50,000 require three competitive quotes.

**3.2** A single-source purchase must be justified in writing and approved by a director.

**3.3** Quotes must be kept with the purchase record.

## 4. Approval authority

**4.1** Purchases are approved by the person named in Table 4.1, based on the purchase value.

**Table 4.1** Approval authority for purchases

| Purchase value | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to CAD 5,000 | Department manager |
| CAD 5,001 to CAD 25,000 | Director |
| Over CAD 25,000 | Vice president |

**4.2** A purchase must not be split into smaller orders to stay under an approval limit.

## 5. Suppliers

**5.1** New suppliers must pass a due diligence check before the first order is placed.

**5.2** Suppliers must accept the company supplier code of conduct.

**5.3** Supplier performance is reviewed once a year for contracts that run longer than one year.

## 6. Corporate travel purchases

**6.1** Employees must book flights through the approved travel portal.

**6.2** Negotiated airline and hotel rates are managed by the Procurement team and published in the portal.

## 7. Vehicle and equipment hire

**7.1** This policy governs all vehicle rentals, and all rental agreements must be arranged by the Procurement team.

**7.2** Ground support equipment hired for airport operations follows the same approval rules as any other purchase.

## 8. Payment

**8.1** Suppliers are paid against an approved invoice and a confirmed delivery.

**8.2** Advance payments require approval from the finance director.
