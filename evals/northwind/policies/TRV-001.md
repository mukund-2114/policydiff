# Travel Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

**1.1** This policy sets out how Northwind Air employees and contractors plan, book and pay for travel on company business.

**1.2** It applies to all employees and to contractors who travel at the company's expense.

**1.3** Training and conference travel is governed by this policy in the same way as any other business trip.

## 2. Approval and booking

**2.1** Every business trip requires written approval from the employee's line manager before any booking is made, regardless of cost.

**2.2** Bookings must be made through the approved travel portal.

**2.3** An approved itinerary that changes in cost or destination must be approved again by the line manager.

## 3. Flights

**3.1** Economy class is the default for all flights.

**3.2** Business class is not permitted on any flight, for any employee, under any circumstances.

**3.3** Employees should choose the lowest logical fare that meets the business need.

**3.4** Loyalty points earned on business travel may be kept by the employee.

## 4. Accommodation, meals and ground transport

### 4.1 Hotels

**4.1.1** Hotel rates are capped at CAD 220 per night in major cities.

**4.1.2** Hotels should be chosen from the preferred hotel list where one is available for the destination.

### 4.2 Meals

**4.2.1** Meals on trips are reimbursed as a daily per diem. The rate for each city is listed in Table 4.1.

**Table 4.1** Daily meal per diem by city

| City | Daily per diem |
| --- | --- |
| Toronto | CAD 95 |
| Montreal | CAD 85 |
| Vancouver | CAD 90 |
| Calgary | CAD 80 |
| Halifax | CAD 75 |

**4.2.2** Alcohol is not reimbursed under any circumstances.

### 4.3 Ground transport

**4.3.1** Use of a personal vehicle for business travel is reimbursed at CAD 0.68 per kilometre.

**4.3.2** Taxis and ride-hailing services may be used when public transport is not practical.

## 5. Expense claims

**5.1** Travel expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the end of the trip.

**5.2** Claims are submitted using Form EX-12 in the finance portal.

**5.3** Each claim must include the approved itinerary and the approval record.

**5.4** Receipts are required for any expense over CAD 25.

## 6. Vehicle rentals

**6.1** This policy governs all vehicle rentals used for business travel, and employees book rental cars through the travel portal.

**6.2** The company purchases rental insurance. Employees must decline optional coverage offered at the rental counter.

## 7. Safety while travelling

**7.1** Travellers must carry the company emergency contact number for the duration of the trip.

**7.2** Any injury, theft or serious disruption during a trip must be reported to the line manager as soon as it is safe to do so.
