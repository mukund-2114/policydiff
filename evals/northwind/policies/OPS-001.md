# Flight Operations Manual Summary

## 1. Purpose and scope

**1.1** This summary gives the main operating rules from the Flight Operations Manual to dispatchers and flight crew.

**1.2** If this summary and the full manual differ, the full manual applies.

## 2. Rest requirements

**2.1** The minimum rest between duty periods is listed in Table 2.1.

**Table 2.1** Minimum rest between duty periods

| Sector type | Minimum rest |
| --- | --- |
| Short-haul | 10 hours |
| Long-haul | 14 hours |

**2.2** Rest must be taken in suitable accommodation away from the aircraft and the terminal.

## 3. Crew conduct

**3.1** Crew may not operate a flight within 8 hours of consuming alcohol.

**3.2** Crew must be fit for duty and must not fly while taking medication that affects alertness.

## 4. Duty limits

**4.1** The maximum flight duty period for long-haul pilots is 12 hours.

**4.2** A captain may extend a duty period only with the approval of Operations Control.

## 5. Dispatch and weather

**5.1** Dispatchers must give the crew current weather and airport conditions before every departure.

**5.2** A flight must not depart if the weather is below the minimums published for the destination and alternate airports.

## 6. Records

**6.1** Duty and rest records are retained for 3 years.

**6.2** Records must be available to the regulator on request.
