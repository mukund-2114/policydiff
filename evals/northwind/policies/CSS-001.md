# Customer Service Standards

## 1. Purpose, scope and definitions

**1.1** This document gives airport and call centre agents the standards they follow when helping passengers.

**1.2** It applies to all customer-facing agents, including contracted staff.

**1.3** A delay is a departure 2 hours or more after the scheduled time.

## 2. Baggage guidance for agents

**2.1** Agents use the allowances in Table 2.1 when they answer passenger questions.

**Table 2.1** Baggage allowance summary for agents

| Fare class | Bags | Weight per bag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Economy | 1 | 20 kg |
| Premium Economy | 2 | 23 kg |
| Business | 2 | 32 kg |

**2.2** A cabin bag must not exceed 55 x 40 x 20 cm.

**2.3** The excess baggage fee is CAD 100 per bag above the allowance.

## 3. Refunds

**3.1** Agents quote the refund processing times in Table 3.1.

**Table 3.1** Refund processing times for agents

| Payment method | Processing time |
| --- | --- |
| Credit card | 10 business days |
| Debit card | 7 business days |
| Travel voucher | 2 business days |

**3.2** Passengers who cancel within 24 hours of booking are entitled to a full refund.

## 4. Complaints

**4.1** Complaints are acknowledged within 2 business days.

**4.2** Agents record every complaint in the customer system, even if it is resolved on the spot.

## 5. Service behaviour

**5.1** Agents greet every passenger politely and listen before they offer a solution.

**5.2** Agents escalate to a supervisor when a passenger asks for one.
