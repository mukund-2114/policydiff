# Data Retention Policy

## 1. Purpose, scope and definitions

**1.1** This policy sets how long Northwind Air keeps records and how they are disposed of.

**1.2** The Compliance team is responsible for approving the deletion of customer data.

**1.3** Confidential data means customer personal data, financial records and employee records.

## 2. Retention schedule

**2.1** Financial records are retained for 7 years.

**2.2** Customer booking records are retained for 5 years after the last flight date.

**2.3** Records must be kept in the system of record and not copied to unmanaged locations.

**2.4** A legal hold overrides all retention schedules.

## 3. Employee records

**3.1** Personnel files are retained for 7 years after employment ends.

**3.2** Records of unsuccessful job applicants are retained for 1 year.

## 4. Operational records

**4.1** Crew duty records are retained for 3 years.

**4.2** Aircraft maintenance records are retained for the life of the aircraft plus 2 years.

## 5. Disposal

**5.1** Records that reach the end of their retention period must be securely deleted or destroyed.

**5.2** A record of each disposal must be kept by the team that carried it out.

**5.3** Backup copies are removed according to the normal backup cycle.
