# Refunds and Cancellations Policy

## 1. Purpose and definitions

**1.1** This policy explains when Northwind Air refunds a fare and how long a refund takes.

**1.2** A business day is any day from Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.

**1.3** A delay is a departure more than 3 hours after the scheduled time.

## 2. Passenger cancellations

**2.1** Passengers may cancel within 24 hours of booking for a full refund.

**2.2** Cancellations after that period are refunded according to the rules of the fare that was bought.

**2.3** Non-refundable fares may be changed for a fee, if the fare rules allow it.

## 3. Refund processing

**3.1** Refunds are returned to the original form of payment. Processing times are listed in Table 3.1.

**Table 3.1** Refund processing times

| Payment method | Processing time |
| --- | --- |
| Credit card | 7 business days |
| Debit card | 7 business days |
| Travel voucher | 2 business days |

**3.2** Customers who paid with more than one method are refunded to each method in proportion.

## 4. Cancellations and delays by the airline

**4.1** When Northwind Air cancels a flight, the passenger may choose a full refund or a seat on the next available flight.

**4.2** Passengers affected by a delay are offered meals and, where needed, hotel accommodation.

**4.3** Compensation for delays and cancellations follows the Canadian Air Passenger Protection Regulations.

## 5. Complaints about refunds

**5.1** Refund complaints are acknowledged within 2 business days.

**5.2** Complaints that cannot be resolved by the first agent are escalated to a supervisor.
