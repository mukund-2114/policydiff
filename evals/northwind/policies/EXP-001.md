# Expense Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

**1.1** This policy explains which business expenses Northwind Air reimburses and how employees claim them.

**1.2** It applies to all employees. Contractors follow the terms of their contract.

**1.3** Where a travel-specific rule exists, the Travel Policy explains how the trip itself is planned and booked.

## 2. Submitting claims

**2.1** The company reimburses approved business expenses after the employee submits a claim.

**2.2** Every claim must state the business purpose of each expense.

**2.3** Purchases and travel costs under CAD 500 do not require prior approval.

**2.4** Form EX-12 was withdrawn on 1 September 2024. All claims must be filed on Form EX-14 in the finance portal.

## 3. Allowances

**3.1** Meals while travelling are reimbursed as a daily per diem. The rate for each city is listed in Table 3.1.

**Table 3.1** Daily meal per diem by city

| City | Daily per diem |
| --- | --- |
| Toronto | CAD 85 |
| Montreal | CAD 85 |
| Vancouver | CAD 90 |
| Calgary | CAD 80 |
| Halifax | CAD 75 |

**3.2** Meals during normal work days at the employee's usual work location are not reimbursed.

**3.3** Employees receive a home office equipment allowance of CAD 500 once every three years.

## 4. Travel-related expenses

**4.1** Hotel stays are reimbursed up to CAD 220 per night.

**4.2** Economy class is the standard fare for flights.

**4.3** Business class may be booked for flights longer than 6 hours with director approval.

**4.4** Use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at CAD 0.68 per kilometre.

## 5. Claim deadlines and approvals

**5.1** Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

**5.2** Late claims require approval from the finance director.

**5.3** Receipts are required for any expense over CAD 25.

**5.4** Claims are approved by the person named in Table 5.1, based on the claim total.

**Table 5.1** Approval limits for expense claims

| Claim total | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to CAD 2,500 | Department manager |
| CAD 2,501 to CAD 10,000 | Director |
| Over CAD 10,000 | Vice president |

## 6. Purchases and gifts

**6.1** Any single purchase over CAD 50,000 must follow the competitive quote process in the Procurement Policy.

**6.2** Gifts to customers valued over CAD 100 must be recorded in the gift register.

**6.3** Gifts to government officials are not permitted.

## 7. Records

**7.1** Receipts and expense records are kept for 6 years.

**7.2** Records are stored in the finance portal and must not be kept only on personal devices.
