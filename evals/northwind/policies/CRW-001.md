# Crew Rest and Duty Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

**1.1** This policy sets the rest and duty limits that protect Northwind Air crew from fatigue.

**1.2** It applies to all pilots and cabin crew.

**1.3** This policy replaces Safety Policy SAF-002, which was withdrawn on 15 January 2025.

## 2. Rest between duties

**2.1** Crew must have the minimum rest in Table 2.1 between the end of one duty period and the start of the next.

**Table 2.1** Minimum rest between duty periods

| Sector type | Minimum rest |
| --- | --- |
| Short-haul | 12 hours |
| Long-haul | 14 hours |

**2.2** Crew must not report for duty within 8 hours of consuming alcohol.

**2.3** Rest periods must be spent free from company duties, including phone calls and training.

## 3. Duty limits

**3.1** The maximum flight duty period for each crew type is shown in Figure 3.1.

![Maximum flight duty period by crew type](figures/crew_duty_hours.png)

**Figure 3.1** Maximum flight duty period by crew type

**3.2** Scheduling must not plan a duty period beyond the limit. Extensions are only for unforeseen operating conditions.

## 4. Standby and reserve

**4.1** Crew on standby must be able to reach the airport within the reporting time set by Crew Scheduling.

**4.2** Standby time that turns into flight duty counts towards the duty limit.

## 5. Fatigue

**5.1** Any crew member may declare themselves too fatigued to fly without penalty.

**5.2** Crew Scheduling must record each fatigue declaration and review repeated declarations for patterns.

## 6. Reporting

**6.1** Crew must report safety incidents within 24 hours.

**6.2** Reports are made through the incident portal described in the Incident Reporting Policy.
