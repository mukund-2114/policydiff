# Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose, scope and definitions

**1.1** This policy describes the paid and unpaid leave available to Northwind Air employees.

**1.2** It applies to all full-time and part-time employees. Casual staff follow the terms of their agreement.

**1.3** A business day is any day from Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

## 2. Vacation

**2.1** Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per year.

**2.2** Vacation requests are approved by the line manager, based on operational needs.

**2.3** Unused vacation may be carried over into the next year, up to 5 days.

**2.4** Vacation for part-time employees is calculated in proportion to their hours.

## 3. Sick leave

**3.1** Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year.

**3.2** A medical certificate may be requested for an absence of three or more consecutive business days.

**3.3** Employees must tell their line manager before the start of their shift if they cannot work because of illness.

## 4. Bereavement leave

**4.1** Employees may take up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member.

**4.2** Leave for the death of an extended family member is limited to 1 paid day.

**4.3** Additional unpaid time may be agreed with the line manager.

## 5. Parental leave

**5.1** The company tops up parental leave benefits for up to 16 weeks.

**5.2** Employees should tell their line manager and Human Resources of their planned leave dates as early as they can.

**5.3** Employees keep their seniority and benefits coverage during parental leave.

## 6. Unpaid leave

**6.1** Unpaid leave of absence may be requested for personal reasons and requires approval from the line manager and Human Resources.

**6.2** Benefits coverage during unpaid leave is described in the benefits booklet.
